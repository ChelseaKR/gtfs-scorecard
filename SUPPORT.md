# Support

Two different things live under the word "support" here. Pick the one you need.

## Getting help with a feed

- **Something looks wrong on a scorecard:** open a
  [listing correction or claim](https://github.com/ChelseaKR/gtfs-scorecard/issues/new?template=claim-agency.yml).
- **Score a feed that is not listed:** request a
  [one-off score](https://github.com/ChelseaKR/gtfs-scorecard/issues/new?template=score-a-feed.yml),
  or add the agency by following [docs/add-your-agency.md](docs/add-your-agency.md).
- **Not sure whether it is a bug:** ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/ChelseaKR/gtfs-scorecard/discussions) first.
- **Bug report or feature request:** use the
  [issue templates](https://github.com/ChelseaKR/gtfs-scorecard/issues/new/choose).
- **Security vulnerability:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report it
  privately rather than in a public issue.

## Supporting the project financially

The scorecard is free for every transit agency, and it stays that way. An agency
never pays to be scored or to see its fixes. Sponsorship covers the cost of
running the service for everyone: hosting and bandwidth for the site, the read
API and the grade badges, the scheduled daily data refresh, validator compute,
and the evidence retention that makes a published check reproducible.

Sponsorship buys acknowledgment and deliberately little else. It does not buy
influence over grades, methodology, or which agencies are listed, and it never
buys placement. The full rubric is public in [docs/rubric.md](docs/rubric.md).

What running the service costs, and what stays free no matter what, is spelled
out on the [support page](https://gtfsscorecard.org/support/) and in
[docs/support.md](docs/support.md).

**No payment rail is live yet.** Sponsorship accounts are still being set up, so
nothing here points at a checkout that does not exist. To start a conversation:

- [Open a sponsorship inquiry](https://github.com/ChelseaKR/gtfs-scorecard/issues/new?title=Sponsorship%20inquiry), or
- write to [Chelsea Kelly-Reif](mailto:ckellyreif@gmail.com).

## Paid services

Programs and consultancies that want a facilitated audit, a staff workshop, or
implementation help can review the current
[consulting services and pricing](https://chelseakr.com/consulting/). Paid help
is separate from scoring. It never changes a grade, and nothing is subtracted
from the free tier to create it.
