# Bias and disparate-impact review

**Date:** 2026-07-10  
**Unit assessed:** a published GTFS feed, never an employee or rider.

## Distributional risks

- Small, rural, tribal, seasonal, and volunteer-run systems have fewer vendor and staff
  resources, and are less likely to publish realtime.
- Shared regional feeds can make ownership ambiguous.
- Coverage is strongest in the US and Canada; missing geography is not evidence of low
  quality.
- Accessibility fields can be absent because an export omits known information, not
  because vehicles or stops are inaccessible.

## Safeguards

- Missing realtime receives no zero and does not lower the overall score.
- Percentiles include size-peer context and do not replace the underlying evidence.
- Public ranked lists require current, fully measured records and a minimum cohort.
- Accessibility findings say what the feed states and explicitly do not certify usability.
- Identity fields support canonical feeds, aliases, shared operators, official status,
  and evidence-backed corrections.
- Equity overlays are descriptive support-prioritization tools, not funding or service
  eligibility rules.

## Monitoring

Review score distributions and exclusion reasons by size band, geography, feed status,
and official/alias status after rubric or catalog changes. Investigate a material shift
before publishing a new methodology version.
