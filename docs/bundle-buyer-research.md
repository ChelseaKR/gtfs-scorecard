# Program report bundle: buyer research

Research deliverable for the program tier (`docs/program-plan.md`). Not outreach
copy, not a mailing list to run through — a list for the owner to review and
work from in her own voice. Nothing on it has been contacted.

## Who this is for

Per `docs/program-plan.md`, the buyer is never a single agency (agency-facing
stays free). It is an organization that stands in front of *many* agencies at
once and would want one branded archive instead of building one report per
agency by hand:

- a **state DOT public-transit division** that collects GTFS or performance
  data from every transit provider in the state and has to summarize it for a
  legislature, a board, or a federal reporting cycle;
- a **federally funded Rural Transit Assistance Program (RTAP) or technical-
  assistance center** that supports a roster of small/rural operators and
  reports on program-wide outcomes;
- a **consultancy or data vendor** already under contract to prepare GTFS,
  planning, or compliance deliverables across a state's or region's transit
  agencies, for whom a per-agency report is a subcontracted or in-house line
  item today.

## Method and caveats

Compiled 2026-09-17 from each organization's own public site (program pages,
staff/contact pages, FTA and state-DOT program documentation). Every entry
below was verified to currently exist and currently do the described work;
nothing here is inferred from a stale cache. Contacts are general program or
office contact paths — a published inbox, contact form, or main line — not
personal emails guessed from a name pattern. Two things to know before using
this:

- **Named staff contacts are attributed to a role, not guessed.** Where an
  organization's own site names a specific program director or office contact
  as *the* way to reach that program (e.g., a state RTAP's listed director),
  that person's published contact is used because it is the org's own
  answer to "who do I write to," not a guess. Confirm the name is still
  current before sending anything — these turn over.
- **One organization, "Small Urban and Rural Center on Mobility" (NDSU/UGPTI),
  was deliberately left off this list.** It surfaced in research as a
  National-RTAP-funded rural transit research center, but its own materials
  say the program is no longer active due to funding and staffing changes.
  Listing a defunct program as a live lead would be the "absence rendered as
  a value" failure mode this portfolio watches for.
