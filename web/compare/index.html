Compare two agencies.

Choose two scorecards to check whether they use the same rubric, scoring profile, validator, and measured category set, and come from distinct feed bytes. Like-for-like results can appear in one table; otherwise the page keeps the grades separate and links to both full scorecards.

Both lists start with 50 of 2176 agencies. The rest arrive when you reach for a picker, or load them now. Browse the paginated agency directory.

Category scores measure published data quality, not service quality, and a missing realtime feed is shown as not yet published, never as a failure. How to read a scorecard.
