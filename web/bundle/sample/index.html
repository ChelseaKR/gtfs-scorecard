Top three things to fix

Three improvements, in priority order, each sized so a board can see what it is approving:

  1. Set wheelchair_boarding to 1 (accessible) or 2 (not accessible) for every stop. A field survey can start with the busiest stops.

    296 of 296 stops don't say whether a wheelchair user can board there. Riders who use wheelchairs can't plan a trip when accessibility is marked 'unknown'; apps show no information at all.

    Estimated effort: A column in stops.txt; your scheduling software likely has it.

  2. Set wheelchair_accessible on every trip. If every vehicle is accessible, this may be one default; otherwise use the value for each trip.

    1794 of 1794 trips don't say whether the vehicle is wheelchair accessible. Even with accessible stops, riders need to know the bus itself can take them.

    Estimated effort: A default or per-trip field in your export.

  3. Turn on 'remove unused shapes' (or the like) in your export tool.

    The feed contains route shapes no trip uses. Harmless to riders, but it bloats the feed and suggests stale export data.

    Estimated effort: One setting.
