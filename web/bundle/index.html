Board reports for every agency you support

GTFS Scorecard grades a transit agency's published GTFS feed for data quality and rider-information gaps, free, for any agency, at gtfsscorecard.org. A state program, a technical-assistance center, a feed vendor, or a consultancy preparing packets for many agencies can get every agency's board report in one archive, with the program's own name, logo, and accent on each cover, and refreshed monthly if you want. Each file is the same self-contained report the site already ships for one agency; the bundle is the packaging.

  • What arrives? One self-contained board report per agency id you name, in a single archive, with a manifest that accounts for every id you asked for.
  • What branding does it carry? Your program's name, logo, and accent color on every cover.
  • When does it arrive? Normally within the hour, always within two business days. If it is later than that, the purchase is refunded. That is a commitment, not an estimate.
  • What if the archive is wrong, or something about the order is not right? Reply to the receipt Stripe emailed you, or open an issue on GitHub, and it will be rebuilt or refunded.
  • How long does the download link live? 30 days. A monthly plan sends a fresh archive and a fresh link each month until you cancel from the receipt Stripe emailed you.
  • Do the monthly and yearly refresh plans renew automatically? Yes. Each renews automatically, at the price and interval you chose, until you cancel from the receipt Stripe emailed you when you bought the bundle. Canceling stops the next charge and does not affect an archive already sent.
  • What does a purchase change about a grade? Nothing. Grades, methodology, and which agencies are listed are not for sale, and an agency's own report stays free.
  • Who built this, and how do I reach them? Chelsea Kelly-Reif, the same person who built and maintains the free scorecard, starting from the transit systems in Davis, California. Questions before you buy: open an issue on GitHub. More about the project.

Plans and prices

What stays free?

Everything an agency touches, including this report for its own feed. Open any agency page and choose the board one-pager, or generate the same file yourself with the open-source command. Nothing is subtracted from the free tier to create the bundle; it is an addition for the people who manage many agencies at once, and it is never the way an agency sees its own grade.

How it works

  1. Choose a plan above and pay through Stripe. GTFS Scorecard never sees your card.
  2. Stripe sends you to a short form: program name, an accent color, an optional logo, the agency ids you support (up to the number the plan you bought covers), and where to send the archive.
  3. The reports render from the published scorecards and the download link goes to that address, normally within the hour and always within two business days. If it is later than that, the purchase is refunded.
  4. The link stays valid for 30 days. A monthly plan sends a fresh archive with a fresh link every month until you cancel from the receipt Stripe emailed you.

A monthly or yearly refresh renews a bundle you have already bought. It re-sends that archive, refreshed, for the same agencies the bundle covered, and it does not include a bundle of its own: buy a bundle first, with the same email address, then start the refresh. A refresh with no bundle behind it is refused before the checkout is used, and says so, so nothing is built and nothing is set up on a checkout you can still cancel.

Every archive includes a manifest that names each id you asked for and what happened to it. An id that is not tracked, a retired record, or an agency with no published scorecard yet is listed with the reason, never silently dropped.

What does a purchase not buy?

No influence over the scores. Grades, methodology, weights, and which agencies are listed are never for sale, and the bundle's numbers are the same ones on the public site. Every report keeps its attribution to the open-source scorecard and cites the public rubric. Sponsorship or purchase buys no influence over grades, methodology, or which agencies are listed.

Prices are in US dollars and are set on the server, not on this page. The build runs in the open-source repository's own automation and the archive is stored privately behind a link only your email receives.
